让记忆拥有形状。我们通过软件、视觉创作和文化项目， 把日常生活转化成可以被记录、重新遇见，并最终拥有实体形态的记忆。

Making Memory Tangible. We create software, visual experiences and cultural projects that turn everyday life into memories that can be captured, revisited and eventually given physical form.

关于 ABOUT
ABOUT

每一天都会留下很多痕迹：我们去了哪里，把注意力放在了什么地方， 听见了什么声音，以及哪些人、动物和地方曾经短暂经过我们的生活。 我们通过软件、绘画、声音、故事和实体设计，把这些容易消失的痕迹留下来。

Every day leaves traces: where we went, what held our attention, what we heard, and the people, animals and places that crossed our lives. Shanghai Bird explores ways of capturing them through software, drawing, sound, storytelling and physical design.

方法 METHOD
METHOD
  • 捕捉 Capture：记录注意力、情绪、声音、地方和日常生活留下的痕迹。
  • 转译 Interpret：把痕迹变成时间线、绘画、故事、地图与视觉形式。
  • 保存 Preserve：让它们在未来仍可以重新被发现。
  • 实体化 Materialize：把部分数字记忆转译成书籍、物件和实体形式。
  • Capture: traces from attention, feeling, sound, place and everyday life.
  • Interpret: timelines, drawings, stories, maps and visual forms.
  • Preserve: keep them available for future rediscovery.
  • Materialize: translate selected digital memories into books, objects and physical forms.

城市与文化 CITY & CULTURE
CITY & CULTURE

上海不仅是我们工作的地方，它本身也是我们长期观察的对象。 SH飞行是一个从上海出发的城市与鸟类观察计划，记录飞来飞去的人、鸟和故事。 我们通过鸟类、迁徙、步行、环境录音、聊天与城市观察，记录人与动物、人与城市之间不断变化的关系。

Shanghai is not only where we work. It is one of our subjects. SH Flight is a city and bird observation project starting from Shanghai, documenting people, birds and stories in motion through walking, field recordings, conversations and urban observation.

实体记忆 PHYSICAL MEMORY
PHYSICAL MEMORY

当一段数字记忆离开屏幕，会发生什么？一年的注意力轨迹可以成为一本书， 一系列漫画可以成为个人档案，一段声音可以变成一种形状，一组数据可以成为一个物件。 这是 Shanghai Bird 正在探索的未来方向，不是对具体产品的承诺。

What happens when a digital memory leaves the screen? A year of attention might become a book, a collection of comics a personal archive, a sound a shape, and a dataset an object. This is a future direction we are exploring, not a promise of specific products.
