关于 ABOUT
ABOUT

我们关注人在日常生活中如何观察、记录和理解自己的经验。 项目横跨软件产品、视觉设计、声音记录与城市观察， 尝试用更轻、更具体的方式保存那些容易被忽略的过程。

We explore how people observe, record and understand their everyday experiences. Our work spans software products, visual design, field recordings and urban observation, preserving processes that are often overlooked.

Moodlecat

画一只猫，保存一种心情。

Draw a cat. Save a feeling.

一款以放松涂鸦和情绪记录为核心的创意应用。 用户可以画下当天的猫咪，把它放入时间胶囊， 在未来某一天重新打开当时的心情。

A gentle doodling app for capturing everyday feelings. Draw a cat, place it in a time capsule, and rediscover that moment in the future.

  • 情绪记录

OrbitLog

看见一天中注意力去过哪里。

See where your attention goes.

一款用于观察个人注意力轨迹的应用。 它记录专注、休息、中断和返回， 帮助使用者回顾注意力如何进入、离开并重新回到任务。

A private app for observing the movement of attention. It records focus, breaks, distractions and returns, helping users understand how attention moves through the day.

  • 专注记录
  • 注意力时间线
  • 中断与返回
  • Focus sessions
  • Attention timeline
  • Distractions and returns

SH飞行 SH Flight

上海、鸟类、迁徙与城市记忆。

Shanghai, birds, migration and urban memory.

一个从上海出发的城市文化观察项目。 通过散步、鸟类观察、录音、播客和声音地图， 记录城市里人与鸟的停留、离开和再次相遇。

An urban cultural project from Shanghai. Through walks, bird observation, field recordings, podcasts and sound maps, it documents how people and birds stay, leave and meet again in the city.

  • 城市观察
  • 鸟类与迁徙
  • 播客与声音地图
  • Urban observation
  • Birds and migration
  • Podcast and sound map
